VeriScan

Blockchain-Integrated Digital Forensics Platform

Final Year Project Presentation

The Digital Problem

With the rise of AI tools and cyber attacks, it is getting harder to tell what is real and what is fake online.

AI vs. Original Content

It is increasingly difficult to determine if content is generated by AI or is original. For academic purposes, this creates a high risk of undetected plagiarism.

Security Risks

Fake videos (deepfakes), scam web links, and hidden viruses are tricking people easily every day.

The Solution: VeriScan

A single platform with two different modes to help people find fake and dangerous content.

Academic Mode

Simple tools for teachers to quickly scan student work to see if it was written by AI or copied from the internet.

Security Mode

Advanced tools to spot fake videos, check dangerous web links, and find hidden information in files.

Smart App Design

Why build two different apps when everything can be in one place?

  • Easy Switching: Change from Academic Mode to Security Mode with just one click.
  • Visually Appealing UI: The app turns Purple for school tools and Green for security tools so you always know where you are.
  • Perfect Navigation: The menu automatically changes to show only the tools you need right now.

Why Use Blockchain?

We need our results to be perfectly secure. Once data is saved on the blockchain, it cannot be changed.

Permanent Records

Every scan result, threat detected, or copied work is saved properly so it can never be deleted.

Proof of Originality

Safe documents get a special digital stamp. If anyone tries to change the document later, the stamp breaks to warn everyone.

Technology Behind The App
